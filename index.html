Who We Are

The apex body uniting Bangladesh's pharmaceutical manufacturers

Since 1972, BAPI has worked closely with regulators and industry to promote GMP standards, export growth, and access to affordable medicine.

Research & quality control
Our Role

BAPI represents the pharmaceutical manufacturers of Bangladesh, working hand-in-hand with the Directorate General of Drug Administration and international bodies to uphold Good Manufacturing Practice, expand export access, and champion the industry's global reputation for quality, affordable generics.

From insulin and hormones to anti-cancer therapies, member companies now produce nearly every major drug category — a journey from import dependency to becoming one of Asia's leading exporters of quality medicine.

Prospect & Opportunities

The competitive edge of Bangladesh Pharma

Cost, capability and compliance — the three pillars behind Bangladesh's rise as a trusted generics exporter.

Cost advantage

Labour costs 3–4x lower than India and China, keeping quality generics globally price-competitive.

TRIPS advantage

LDC-status patent flexibilities let manufacturers produce patented generics ahead of global peers.

Diverse dosage skill

Deep experience across tablets, injectables, biosimilars and complex delivery systems.

GMP & global compliance

Facilities certified to WHO, US FDA, UK MHRA and EU GMP standards across multiple manufacturers.

CMO-ready capacity

Proven contract manufacturing experience, ready to scale for international partners.

API Park ahead

A dedicated 200-acre park will soon localize raw ingredient supply for the whole industry.

Manufacturing Excellence

Built on global-standard quality systems

BAPI member facilities operate under rigorous quality management — from raw material sourcing to finished dosage — meeting the standards required to serve regulated markets worldwide.
